Dear Abby...

Before anything else, I want you to know that this little corner of the internet was made with you in mind.

Take your time reading. There's no rush.

♡

01. First, about yesterday...

I'm really sorry I couldn't call you yesterday. There's something I want you to understand.

I have asthma, and although I've been getting better, there's still a possibility of having serious episodes. It's something I've always found difficult to talk about because, honestly, it's one of my biggest insecurities. That's why I once told you I used to have asthma.

Yesterday, while I was trying to call you, things got a little complicated. I wasn't dressed warmly enough, my breathing became worse, and my inhaler was almost finished. Thankfully, my brother brought me another one.

I hope you understand why I couldn't continue with the call. I wasn't avoiding you, and I certainly wasn't unwilling to be there for you.

🩷

02. About you, darling...

I don't want you to suffer. That's one of the reasons I always try to brighten your day, even when things aren't particularly easy.

"When you started dancing in the rain, I honestly wished I could have been there."

I would have wanted to pull you into a warm embrace and remind you that everything would eventually be okay.

I know yesterday wasn't easy for you, and I'm genuinely sorry you had to go through so much.

I might not always know the right words to say, and sometimes I might not be able to do much, but I genuinely try. Your happiness matters to me, and knowing that you're hurting isn't something I can simply overlook.

Please remember that you don't have to carry everything alone. You can talk to me, even when you don't know where to begin.

🌧️ ♡ 🌷

03. About your parents...

I know I might sound like I'm parenting you sometimes, but I'm only sharing what I genuinely believe could help.

I understand that emotions can get overwhelming, and sometimes things come out differently from how we intended. But please don't let yesterday's situation create distance between you and your parents.

When you're ready, perhaps you could approach them, apologise for what happened and explain how you were feeling. You don't have to pretend that everything was okay, but taking responsibility and communicating honestly could make a difference.

Remember, they're still your parents, and you're still their child. Even when things get difficult, the love between you doesn't simply disappear.

And please don't feel that running away from the situation is your only option. I understand wanting some space because I've experienced difficult moments too. But sometimes, facing things gently is the beginning of making them better.

I believe you can take that first step.

🕊️

04. A little reminder of who you are...

I know I might sometimes sound as though I'm trying to tell you how to live your life, but that's never my intention.

I see you as the loving, caring and kind-hearted person you've always shown me. One difficult day doesn't erase all those beautiful qualities.

"We don't need to be perfect; we just need to keep becoming better."

And please, don't take your health lightly. I know you have asthma too, and I want you to take care of yourself. You deserve to be healthy, happy and at peace.

If you ever feel overwhelmed, remember that you don't have to pretend to be strong all the time.

🌸 💗 🌸
✨

05. Our little reminder of hope

I might be offline for a little while whenever my breathing difficulties leave me feeling weak, but that doesn't mean I've stopped caring.

My body might be weak sometimes, but my hope is strong, and my love for you is even stronger.

"If you ever feel like all hope is lost, remember that we can share mine."

It's big enough for two. 🫶🏽

💗 ✨ 💗

Just one more thing...

You're still the Abby I know: the beautiful, kind-hearted, loving and understanding person who deserves good things.

Please take care of yourself, okay? Not just for anyone else, but because your health and happiness matter.

I hope things get better between you and your parents, and I hope you find the peace you've been needing.

And whenever you're ready, we can talk about everything. No pressure, no pretending, just an honest conversation.

I'll let you know when I'm feeling better.

💗

Remember this, Abby...

You're appreciated, you're cared for, and you don't have to figure everything out in one day.
