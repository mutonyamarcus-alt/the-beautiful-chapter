The letter

A story worth remembering

Some stories are too beautiful to forget...

Sooo, Hiii! 😂❤️

I wanna say something. Actually, a lot of things, but don't worry, I'll try not to bore you. 😂

I'll start with a little storytelling...

It all started when I first saw you at the music festivals. I remember seeing the most beautiful girl in the whole world looking at me. You greeted me so casually, leaving me completely speechless. You actually had to say that we'd talk later just to bring me back to my senses. 😂❤️ And that's how I started falling for you.

At the time, I hadn't really interacted with many people I didn't know, so I couldn't quite understand what I was feeling. But somehow, I knew I wanted to know you. I wanted to become someone special in your life.

A whole year passed, and the music festival season came around again. This time, I promised myself I'd finally gather the courage to talk to you. But guess what? I kept losing my courage. 😭

I'd even pleaded with our teacher to let me join the presenting team because Rihanna had told me you'd be coming. I was nervous the entire day, but somehow, I miraculously qualified to proceed! 😂

You know what? Since your school didn't proceed, I lost all my motivation to continue. It just didn't feel the same anymore, especially knowing you weren't going to be there.

I still wanted to talk to you and get to know you, but I had absolutely no way of reaching you. So I took a leap of faith and created an Instagram account for the first time in my life. 😂 Suddenly, I found myself in an entirely new universe!

Then came Grade 9. I deleted my account and decided to focus on my studies because I'd made a promise to myself to work hard and build a future I could be proud of.

When the results and school placements came out, I created another account (this very one) because I still wanted a chance to talk to you.

I actually typed a message to you, unsent it, typed another one, and unsent that too. 😭 I just wasn't sure how you'd react.

Eventually, I gathered enough courage and decided that April 1st, 2026, would be the day I'd finally text you.

And we talked. A LOT! 😂❤️

I eventually confessed my feelings on the last day of the holiday because I was genuinely afraid of ruining the little connection we'd built.

From that point onwards, whenever there were school functions, I'd find myself going around looking to see whether your school had arrived. 😂

And just when I was beginning to lose hope, I received that little sticky note you'd sent me.

I read it every single day, and somehow, I never got tired of it. 🥹❤️ That's when I truly began to understand just how much you meant to me.

I even pushed myself harder in my studies because I wanted to make you proud.

When we came back for the holiday, I really wanted us to go on our very first date. I'd actually saved some of my pocket money (KSh 4,000! 😂) specifically because I wanted to take you out and make that day special.

✿   ✧   ✿

And then came the difficult moments.

There was a time when you were going through a particularly stressful period and told me goodbye forever. That night was incredibly difficult for me. I barely slept, and I eventually needed some medical attention before things got better.

From then onwards, I became even more afraid of losing you.

But eventually, things changed. You told me that you wanted us to break up, and although it hurt, I chose to accept your decision. I never wanted to force you to stay somewhere you no longer wished to be.

When you suggested that we remain close friends, I agreed. Even though it wasn't exactly easy for me, I was still genuinely happy whenever I got to hear from you.

But then came another difficult chapter.

Things gradually started changing. Your replies became shorter, sometimes taking an entire day or even longer. I started apologising more often because I genuinely didn't want you to feel like I was bothering you.

I kept hoping that our little friendship would remain intact, but somehow, things continued drifting apart.

And now, look at us... 🥲

Late replies, shorter conversations and a connection that doesn't quite feel the way it used to.

I've thought about it, and I think giving you some space is the right thing to do. I never want my presence to feel like an obligation. If stepping back gives you the peace and space you need, then I'll respect that.

✿   ✧   ✿

Abby, you're genuinely one of the biggest highlights of my year. ❤️

You've introduced me to feelings and experiences I'll always appreciate. Through the happy moments and the difficult ones, I'm grateful that our paths crossed.

I wish things had turned out differently. I wanted to give you that hug I always owed you. I wanted to look into your eyes and tell you just how much you meant to me. I wanted us to make memories that we'd look back on someday and smile about. 🥹

Perhaps some of those little wishes will remain wishes, and that's okay.

Not every beautiful story gets the ending we imagined, but that doesn't mean it wasn't beautiful while it lasted.

At least I got to experience something I'll always be grateful for.

Thank you for being part of my story. Thank you for the laughter, the conversations, the little moments, the sticky note and all the memories we've made.

I'm genuinely glad I met you.

And if there's one thing I hope you never forget, it's that you were appreciated, you mattered, and you made someone's year a little more beautiful.

Take care of yourself, Abby. ❤️🥲
