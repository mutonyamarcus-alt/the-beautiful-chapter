Dear Abby,

A little explanation

First, about yesterday

I'm sorry I didn't manage to call. My asthma has been giving me a difficult time, and honestly, I sometimes feel uncomfortable talking about it. My breathing got worse, partly because I hadn't dressed warmly enough, and my inhaler was almost finished. Thankfully, my brother brought me another one.

I wanted to call and be there for you, especially after the kind of day you had. I hope you understand that my silence wasn't because I didn't care. I did want to talk to you.

A thought about you

About you, darling

When I think about yesterday, I keep hoping that you're doing a little better today. Whatever was going through your mind, I hope you know that you don't have to carry every difficult feeling by yourself.

Please be gentle with yourself. You deserve moments of calm, even when everything around you feels a little overwhelming.

A gentle reminder

About your parents

I know things can get difficult at home, especially when emotions run high. When you're ready, maybe you could talk things through with your parents, explain how you felt, and apologise for the parts you wish had gone differently.

Try not to let one difficult moment create a distance that becomes harder to repair. You deserve to be heard, and they deserve a chance to understand you too. Take things one step at a time.

Please remember

A little reminder of who you are

One difficult day doesn't define you. You're still a kind-hearted person, and one rough moment doesn't erase the good in you. You don't have to have everything figured out, and you don't have to be perfect to be worthy of patience and care.

“We don't need to be perfect; we just need to keep becoming better.”
Something to hold on to

Our little reminder of hope

If you ever feel like all hope is lost, remember that we can share mine. It's big enough for the both of us.

My body might be weak sometimes, but my hope is strong, and my love for you is even stronger. I may need a little time to feel like myself again, but I'll let you know how I'm doing.

And please, take your health seriously too. You matter, Abby. More than you might realise.

Remember this, Abby...

You deserve happiness, peace, understanding, and all the beautiful things life has to offer. Even on the difficult days, there is still room for a better tomorrow.

Until I feel better, take care of yourself. I'll update you when I can.

With care, always. ♡
